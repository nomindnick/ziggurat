# research/ — spike findings & distilled holdings

Findings notes from spikes and experiments, plus academic papers reduced to
operative claims + parameters. These notes are deliverables (a spike closes by
writing one) and the cross-session record of what was learned.

Conventions:
- Filename: short slug matching the plan item (`espn-access.md`, `market-archives.md`).
- Header: date, status (answered / open), and the decision the note supports.
- Record dead ends too — "X is not available, fallback is Y" is a finding.

First planned entries: `espn-access.md` (spike 1.1), `market-archives.md` (spike 1.2).
